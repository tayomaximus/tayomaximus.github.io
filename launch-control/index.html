Launch Control

A ClickUp space for a creative agency with three client launches in the same week. On Friday the board said all three were on track. Monday morning's check found a missing client approval, a developer who had been blocked for four days, and two "FINAL" files in Drive with different discounts. This space shows how each one was caught, decided and recovered.

Built as a working sample. The clients and team are fictional; the problems are the ones every agency meets in a busy launch week.

How the space is built

  1. Status is not health. Every launch carries a Green, Amber or Red health field set after checking reality, separate from "in progress".
  2. Nobody waits without a name and a date. "Waiting on client" and "Blocked" are their own statuses, and each carries who it waits on, since when and who can unblock it.
  3. One file is the truth. Every shipped asset links its source-of-truth Drive file. File names never contain "FINAL".
  4. Dependencies are linked, so a missing approval visibly holds up the print run, the install and the launch behind it.
  5. Comment trails tell the story: Friday's "on track", Saturday's unanswered chase, Monday's catch and the one-page note to leadership.
